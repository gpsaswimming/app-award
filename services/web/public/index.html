Awards & Scholarships

Each summer GPSA recognizes an outstanding graduating swimmer and an outstanding coach. Choose an award below to submit an application or nomination. Submissions go to the league president for review by the sponsoring families.

J. Kyle Hurdle Scholarship

Up to $800 · graduating senior swimmers

For a recently graduated senior (or a swimmer who has completed their final year of GPSA eligibility) advancing their education. Submitted by the applicant.

“What positive impact has summer swimming had on your life? How do you intend to pay this forward?”
